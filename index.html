How the first model works

NBA: 48 minutes, four 12-minute quarters. College men: 40 minutes, two 20-minute halves. Overtime periods: five minutes. Possessions estimated from FGA + FTA coefficient − offensive rebounds + turnovers. Coefficients: NBA .44, college .475.

League fallback pace: NBA 100, college 69 possessions per team. Fallback scoring: 1.13 / 1.04 points per possession. These are explicit uncalibrated assumptions, not current team ratings. Live pace shrinks toward the prior with 12 minutes of prior weight; efficiency uses 30 possessions of prior weight. Shooting percentages are not blindly extrapolated.

10,000 seeded simulations include tempo and efficiency uncertainty, late-foul and blowout scenarios, scoring variance and repeated overtime when tied. Team-specific rotations, fatigue, injury impact, quarter/half simulation and exact play-by-play possession tracking are pending.

Quote costs use complementary order-book bids, one-contract VWAP, depth, spread and the series fee multiplier when verified. Fee: ceil to cent(.07 × quantity × price × (1 − price) × multiplier). No liquidity means no executable quote. Static snapshots become stale after 90 seconds; recommendations always remain blocked while feed latency and model calibration are unverified.
